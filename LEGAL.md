## Hardware and battery disclaimer

BB-8 contains a **lithium-polymer (LiPo) battery**. This app can read voltage and firmware power state but **cannot** diagnose or repair a failing cell.

You are solely responsible for:

- Safe handling and charging of your droid
- Recognizing signs of battery failure (heat, swelling, instant shutdown)
- Complying with local laws regarding battery disposal and toy operation

**Do not puncture, crush, or short a LiPo battery.** Seek professional disposal for damaged packs.

Opening the sealed BB-8 shell may void any remaining warranty and carries risk of damage or injury.
