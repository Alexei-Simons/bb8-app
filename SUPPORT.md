## Common questions

**Is this official Sphero software?**  
No. See [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md).

**Does the app collect my data?**  
No cloud collection. Local Bluetooth preferences only. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

**My BB-8 will not drive off the charger.**  
Often a failing internal battery, not an app bug. See [README - Battery health](README.md#battery-health) and open a hardware support issue if needed.

**Can I contribute without coding?**  
Yes. Clear bug reports, reproduction steps, and protocol notes in issues are very helpful.
